AI Adoption in EU Companies

Exploring the digital divide across countries, company sizes and industries

1. Introduction

Why we look at artificial intelligence in European businesses

Artificial intelligence is no longer only a research topic: many companies now use chatbots, tools that read documents, or machine learning to make forecasts. But not all companies are adopting AI at the same speed. In 2025 about one European enterprise in five used at least one AI technology, compared to fewer than one in ten in 2021.

Our main question is: how does AI adoption vary across European companies, and what differences emerge between countries, company sizes and economic sectors?

The data comes from the Eurostat survey on ICT usage in enterprises. It covers companies with at least 10 employees and does not include the financial sector, agriculture and mining. There is no data for 2022.
